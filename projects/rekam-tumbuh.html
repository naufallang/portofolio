04 of 05, Child growth app concept

Rekam Tumbuh.

An app that helps parents track the growth and development of children aged 0 to 5.

Rekam Tumbuh started with interviews with parents of young children. Two problems kept coming up: parenting advice online often contradicts itself, and keeping track of immunization schedules by hand is hard.

The prototype answers both with growth charts based on WHO standards, reminders, a daily journal, and access to experts. The business model combines a premium subscription with clinic partnerships, aimed at urban parents aged 25 to 35.

Type
Mobile app concept and prototype
Scope
  • User interviews
  • Prototyping
  • Business model
  • SDG 3
Status
Prototype, not publicly released

Key features

  • Growth charts for weight, height, and head circumference against WHO standards
  • Reminders for immunizations and health check-ups
  • A daily journal for activities and developmental milestones
  • Parenting content from experts and consultations with pediatricians
  • Supports SDG 3, Good Health and Well-Being
