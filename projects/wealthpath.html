WealthPath.

A personal finance platform that puts budgeting, net worth, and expert advice in one place.

WealthPath was built as a group project for a human-computer interaction course. The goal was one place to see where money goes, what you own, and when to ask for help.

Users sign in with Google or email, record transactions, set budgets, and track their assets. When they need advice, they can book a paid session with a certified financial advisor and share a summary of their finances for more accurate recommendations.

Type
Group project, Human-Computer Interaction course
Built with
  • React
  • TypeScript
  • TanStack Router
  • Vite
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Firebase
Links
Open live site

Key features

  • Income and expense tracking with weekly, monthly, and yearly charts
  • Monthly budgets per category, with the remaining amount updated in real time
  • Asset tracking for property, gold, stocks, and cash to calculate net worth
  • Advisor booking and live chat during paid sessions
  • Sign-in with Google or email through Firebase Authentication
