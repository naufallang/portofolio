QuizByte.

A timed multiple-choice quiz that helps computer science students review the basics.

QuizByte turns review sessions into short timed rounds. Each round has eight questions on programming, data science, or web development, and every answer is followed by a short explanation.

Points reward both accuracy and speed, best scores are saved per topic, and the whole quiz can be played with the keyboard.

Key features

  • Three topics plus a mixed round drawn from all of them
  • 20-second timer with points for speed and answer streaks
  • Results with accuracy, average answer time, and a full answer review
  • Best score saved per topic, keyboard shortcuts, and dark mode
