01About

A student developer who likes turning data into products.

I'm Muhammad Naufal Hadianto, known as Gp, a Computer Science and Data Science student at the School of Computer Science, BINUS University.

I enjoy work that sits between software engineering and data: building interfaces people like using, and making sense of the data behind them. My projects include a personal finance platform, a browser tool that profiles CSV datasets, and a child growth app validated through interviews with parents.

For my undergraduate thesis, I'm researching how well question answering over financial tables holds up when documents come from noisy OCR.
