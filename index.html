Data tool, personal project

DataGlance

A browser-based tool that profiles any CSV file in seconds. It infers column types, computes summary statistics, and visualizes distributions and relationships, so you can understand a new dataset before writing any analysis code.

  • Automatic type detection for numeric, categorical, date, and text columns
  • Mean, median, standard deviation, missing values, and outliers (1.5 × IQR) per column
  • Pearson correlation heatmap with automatic insights on strong pairs
  • Scatter plot with color grouping, plus a sortable data preview
  • Runs fully in the browser, so files are never uploaded
  • JavaScript
  • SVG charts
  • Statistics
  • CSV parsing

Mobile app concept, prototype

Rekam Tumbuh

An app that helps parents track the growth of children aged 0 to 5. The idea came from interviews: parents told us online parenting advice is contradictory, and tracking immunizations by hand is hard.

  • Growth charts for weight, height, and head size, compared with WHO standards
  • Reminders for immunizations and health check-ups
  • A daily journal for activities and milestones
  • Parenting tips from experts and consultations with doctors

Aimed at urban parents aged 25 to 35, with a premium subscription and clinic partnerships as the business model. Supports SDG 3, Good Health and Well-Being.

QuizByte

A timed multiple-choice quiz for computer science students, covering programming fundamentals, data science, and web development. Every answer is followed by a short explanation, so the quiz works as a study tool, not just a test.

  • 20-second timer with speed bonus and streak points
  • Results screen with accuracy, average answer time, and a full answer review
  • Best score saved per topic, keyboard shortcuts, and dark mode
